# Draft routines v1 — four integrated "super workouts"

> 2026-08-10. Design draft for owner testing — NOT yet app data. Basis:
> docs/research/synthesis.md (five-source evidence), interview.md (profile).
> Owner brief: whole-body every time — cardio, strength, chains, flexibility,
> balance, deep/rehab muscles; fun; NO exercise repeats within a session
> (left/right pairs allowed); physio integrated, heaviest in the 45-min
> routines; qigong skipped for now. Format: 40 s work / 20 s rest throughout
> (holds use the 40 s as hold time). 30-min = 30 intervals; 45-min = 45.
>
> Standing rules baked into every routine: no jumping (Achilles/sesamoid);
> strength stations reach ~RPE 8–9; rear foot instep-flat in deep
> lunges/split squats; push-ups on fists if first-MTP complains; no
> behind-the-neck or loaded end-range abd+ER; calf work slow (2–3 s up, 3 s
> down); springy/loaded calf work never first; breathe through all isometrics.

## W1 — Hinge & Push Strength (30 min, hinge/push emphasis)

Warm-up: 1 march + arm circles · 2 bodyweight squat (deepening) · 3 hip
hinge + reach · 4 double-leg calf raise (easy, slow) · 5 scapular push-up.
Main: 6/7 split squat L/R (DBs, 3 s down) · 8 push-up (3 s down, pause) ·
9/10 single-leg RDL L/R (DBs) · 11/12 one-arm row L/R (7 kg, squeeze) ·
13 glute bridge march · 14 horse-stance goblet hold · 15/16 slow step-up
L/R · 17 bear-plank shoulder taps · 18 suitcase march (7 kg; switch at 20 s)
· 19/20 side plank L/R · 21 two-hand DB swing · 22 backward walking ·
23 dead bug.
Deep/rehab rider: 24/25 single-leg calf raise L/R (slow; add DB when able) ·
26/27 side-lying hip abduction L/R.
Finisher: 28 slow front kicks (alternating, wall support ok) · 29 standing
knee raise above 90° with holds (alternating) · 30 breathing + easy reach
cooldown.

## W2 — Rotation, Lateral & Pull (30 min, rotation/lateral/pull emphasis)

Warm-up: 1 march + shoulder rolls · 2 cat-cow → child reach · 3 90/90 hip
switches · 4 alternating side-bend reach · 5 wall slides.
Main: 6 goblet squat (3 s down) · 7/8 lateral lunge L/R · 9/10 kickstand RDL
L/R · 11 renegade row (knees option) · 12 push-up to side plank ·
13/14 half-kneeling one-arm press L/R (neutral grip) · 15 weighted Russian
twist · 16/17 wood-chop diagonal L/R (7 kg) · 18 bird dog (slow) ·
19/20 Copenhagen adductor short-lever L/R · 21 DB knee-drive march ·
22 plank pull-through · 23 slow mountain climber.
Deep/rehab rider: 24/25 bent-knee calf raise L/R · 26 tibialis raises (wall
lean) · 27 short-foot balance stand.
Finisher: 28/29 slow roundhouse chamber L/R · 30 gate-pose flank stretch
flow.

## W3 — Full-Body Strength & Conditioning (45 min, full integration, biggest physio cluster)

Warm-up (6): march ramp · ankle pumps + easy calf raises · deepening squats
· hinge + reach · 90/90 switches · scapular push-ups.
Cardio-chain wave (8): knee-lift cardio · two-hand DB swing · step-back
lunge → knee drive L · same R · inchworm walkout · shadowbox knee-drive
punches · backward walking · bear crawl fwd/back.
Strength wave (12): split squat L/R · feet-elevated or 1.5-rep push-up ·
single-leg RDL L/R · one-arm row L/R · half-kneeling press L/R · single-leg
glute bridge L/R · horse-stance goblet hold.
Physio cluster (10): weighted single-leg calf raise L/R (straight knee) ·
bent-knee calf raise L/R · side-lying hip abduction L/R · seated psoas raise
with slow lower L/R · side-lying external rotation L/R (light DB, cuff).
Balance & active-flex (6): single-leg balance + short-foot L/R · slow front
kick L/R · long-sitting straight-leg raise L/R.
Cooldown (3): half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch L/R · breathing + reach.

## W4 — Balance & Active Flexibility (45 min, balance/active-flexibility emphasis)

Warm-up (6): march + arm circles · cat-cow flow · standing leg swings
front-back L/R (wall) · lateral squat rock · wall slides · easy calf raises.
Balance wave (6): single-leg balance reach L/R · crane-stance knee-raise
hold L/R · slow step-down L/R.
Strength wave (12): goblet squat 1.5-rep · push-up plus · lateral lunge L/R
· one-arm DB snatch L/R · two-arm bent-over row · suitcase march (switch
20 s) · wall-sit hold · single-leg glute bridge L/R · dead bug.
Deep/physio rider (8): straight-knee weighted calf raise L/R · standing hip
hitch L/R · bird-dog holds · prone Y/T raise · serratus wall slide ·
toe-spread + short-foot.
Active-flex finisher (8): slow side kick L/R · slow roundhouse L/R ·
standing leg-raise hold L/R · seated straddle reach flow · frog rock.
Cooldown (3): hip-flexor stretch L/R · side-bend holds · breathing.

## Known open questions for review

   mornings (research says demanding strength early after ramp — W3 puts
   cardio-chains first as extended warm-up; right call?).
2. Copenhagen adductor with the undiagnosed right lateral-hip pain — keep,
   modify, or drop?
3. Weighted Russian twist with combined flexion+rotation — fine for this
   back, or substitute?
4. Are the physio clusters big enough that 1–2 sessions/week + habit calf
   dosing genuinely covers the rehab frequency need?
5. Interval sizing: is 40/20 right for near-failure strength stations at
   RPE 8–9, or do key stations need 60/30?
6. Anything here that breaks the fun/no-repeat/whole-body brief or the
   standing safety rules?
