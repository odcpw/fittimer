# Owner interview — profile, constraints, design principles

> Conducted 2026-08-10 in conversation (voice-memo style answers, transcribed
> by the owner). This is the canonical profile all routine design refers to.
> Medical details are self-reported; rehab-adjacent programming should be
> sanity-checked with the treating physiotherapist.

## Profile

- Male, 45, 1.91 m, 87 kg. Two young children (one newborn: interrupted
  sleep, daily baby-carrying).
- Vietnamese-style kung fu 4×/week for years until ~3 years ago; now
  ~1×/week (Tuesday or Friday, varies by his planning).
- Generally fit and flexible (half-way to splits cold).

## Orthopedic history (self-reported)

1. **Achilles tendinopathy, both sides, worse left, MID-portion.** Physio
   prescribed eccentric heel lowers (straight + bent knee) months ago; exact
   dosage forgotten; currently done opportunistically (escalators etc.),
   slowly re-introducing eccentrics. Limits springy/bouncy work; he
   substitutes knee lifts for jumping jacks. Wants spring BACK — restoration,
   not permanent avoidance.
2. **Left shoulder: open capsular repair (~age 23) for recurrent anterior
   dislocation.** Stable for decades; occasional ache/looseness. Comfortable:
   overhead press, DB snatch, push-ups, handstands. Avoids: behind-the-neck,
   loaded end-range abduction+ER. Back-handspring practice Feb 2026 flared it
   for months (now improving).
3. **Left iliopsoas pain history** (at worst couldn't lift leg to tie shoes);
   largely resolved via physio exercise: seated active knee lift with slow
   eccentric lower. Mild bilateral anterior-hip/fascia tightness remains.
4. **Intermittent right upper-buttock/lateral-hip pain**, provoked by long
   sitting/driving. Undiagnosed.
5. **Left foot: early hallux valgus + prior sesamoid irritation** (currently
   calm). Occasional toe cramps when pointing the foot, improving (benign).
6. **Left-right asymmetry in side-bend/lateral-chain flexibility.**

## Logistics & equipment

- ONE guaranteed ~30-min session/week (morning, before work, training cold);
  sometimes a second. Target session lengths: **30 min and 45 min**, plus an
  **optional ~15-min post-workout stretch block**.
- Trains in own dojo room, barefoot on mats. Step/stairs available.
- Equipment: two adjustable dumbbells only — ~4–5 kg and ~7 kg per hand
  (bar + 2×1.25 kg; bar + 2×2.5 kg). Nothing else.
- Timed intervals, default 40 s work / 20 s rest (adjustable later in app).

## Owner decisions (design principles)

- **Restoration, not accommodation**: symmetry, supple fascia, and returned
  spring are positive goals; blocks are progressions toward restored
  capacity, not workarounds.
- **Daily micro-dosing stays habit-anchored** (escalators, stairs, waiting):
  he will NOT open the app for a standalone micro-session. The app carries
  workout sessions only.
- **No metric tracking in the app** (no ROM/splits measurements). Self-tests
  (side-bend reach, kick height, single-leg heel raises) are informal.
- **Full-chain philosophy**: kung-fu-specific in the sense of power through
  muscle chains integrated with core; fluidity; "between yoga, martial arts,
  and strength chains". Conventional-but-effective exercises; nothing too
  exotic. Horse-stance-style isometrics welcome. Knees-over-toes-style knee
  work welcome where evidence supports it.
- **Active flexibility is a goal**: super-slow kicks, leg raises above
  horizontal, chain flexibility + strength generally (not tied to any single
  named method). Evening long-hold stretch routine wanted (yoga-flavored).
- **Interested in a classic long qigong routine** as a session option.
- Wants a dedicated **physio program covering all problem parts** ("get it
  all fixing"), alongside strength (with and without weights), flexibility,
  and stretch routines — final structure per docs/research/synthesis.md.
- Back-handsprings: parked as a conscious luxury decision, not a training
  goal (see research verdicts).
