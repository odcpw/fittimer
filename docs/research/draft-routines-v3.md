# FitTimer W1–W4 v3 contract

Status: implemented contract draft, 2026-08-13. This document records the
roster-first design reviewed by Fable and Grok. Coverage and creator-purity
figures are estimates until the metadata/clip mining bead produces evidence.

## Operator and body rules

- The four identities and lengths stay fixed: Iron Roots 30 intervals, Silk
  Coils 30, Dragon Longform 45, and Crane Longform 45. Every interval remains
  40 seconds work plus 20 seconds rest. Tempo and left/right choice are
  operator-controlled; a visual reminder never prescribes either.
- This is balanced restoration, not avoidance. The sesamoid/hallux constraint
  is modest and trainable: normal strength, calf, foot-intrinsic and deep-stance
  work stay in the program. Use the rear-instep-flat or folded-mat option and
  self-regulate anything that is painful.
- Bilateral mid-portion Achilles loading is a program goal. Calf raises remain
  load-bearing movements. Swings, snatches and kicks come after the warm-up;
  no cold ballistic ankle work and no jumping requirement.
- Left shoulder stabilization remains active: presses, snatches and push-ups
  stay available, while unloaded wall/scapular work remains comfortable and
  there are no behind-neck or loaded end-range abduction/external-rotation
  demands.
- Dragon retains seated psoas raise as a text-cue interval: lift slowly and
  lower with a deliberate eccentric. Right lateral-hip loading remains in the
  palette without claiming that two different patterns are equivalent.

## Visual and text-cue contract

The six approved fitness creators remain the selectable roster: MadFit,
Growingannanas, Caroline Girvan, Sydney Cummings, Heather Robertson and Pamela
Reif. Samery is metadata-only for now; no Samery, Physio Fix, ALTA or
AskDoctorJo media is required by this contract. Shadow Boxing stays dormant.

The following are deliberately text-only: wall-press hip-abduction in Iron and
Silk (about 2 × 15 seconds), the retained Dragon wall-press cue (same text-only
status), seated soleus raise (3 seconds up / 1 second hold / 3 seconds down),
Samery slow kicks/chambers, Dragon seated psoas raise, and Iron backward
walking. A future side-lying hip-abduction pair may be added only as a separate
visual and never presented as equivalent to the wall press.

Coach notes also carry the practical gates: pendulums start small and grow
larger with no snap; swings/snatches/kicks follow the warm-up; rear-instep and
folded-mat options never contradict the shown visual; short-foot means a gentle
arch lift without toe gripping.

## Movement identity changes

- `lateral-step-to-balance-short-foot` is renamed to `lateral-step-to-balance`;
  short-foot is a cue rather than a separate visual identity.
- The old movement records remain in the research catalogue as historical
  variants, but the v3 blocks use the canonical IDs above and the runtime
  media pack has an explicit text fallback for any unresolved movement.
- Push-up variants retain their stable IDs and display labels until the future
  creator-library pack can prove that folding them is safe for voice aliases.
- The MadFit 30/30 single-creator block is unchanged.

## Mining handoff

The next bead is metadata-first over the 16 named roster sources. It downloads
only sources with at least two confirmed v3 palette movements, cuts silent
landscape references/loops with provenance, and leaves unmatched movements
text-only. No timing, side or crop mismatch is a reason to invent a fallback;
the operator can work at a comfortable tempo and choose the side.
