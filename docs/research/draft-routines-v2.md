# Routines v2 — canonical interval lists for implementation

> 2026-08-10. Supersedes draft-routines-v1.md. All oracle-routine-review.md
> edits adopted (W1/W3/W4 ship-with-edits applied; W2 redesigned per its
> replacement). This file is the implementation spec: exercise per interval,
> side, and coach cues. Format: 40 s work / 20 s rest; 30-min = 30 intervals,
> 45-min = 45; the timer keeps the final 20 s rest so sessions hit exactly
> 30:00 / 45:00.

## Global rules (encode as coachNotes / defaults)

- No jumping anywhere; swings/snatches cued "feet planted, no heel pop".
- Toe-safe regressions for ALL floor/plank work: knees-down or hands-elevated
  (NOT fists — fists do not unload the first MTP). Applies to push-ups,
  inchworms, planks.
- Deep lunge/split work: rear instep flat; shorten stance rather than force
  the great toe.
- Calf loading: 3 s up / 1 s pause / 3 s down, never first, never bounced;
  pain 0–3/10 target, 5 ceiling, settled by next morning.
- Shoulder: neutral-grip pressing; no behind-the-neck; stop on apprehension.
- Effort targets: strength stations RPE 8 (occasional 9 on stable stations);
  isometric strength holds RPE 8; calf loading ~RPE 8 within pain rules;
  swings/snatches/shadowboxing RPE 6–7; slow kicks/balance RPE 5–7; cuff,
  short-foot, low-load hip control RPE 5–7.
- Rehab spine (every routine, fixed): ≥1 weighted calf pair + ≥1 lateral-hip
  station + 1 foot or cuff station. 45-min routines carry both knee-angle
  calf pairs + psoas + cuff clusters.
- Warm-ups: ≥6 min on 30-min routines, ankle prep always included (pumps +
  heel-to-toe rocks — NOT calf raises, which would repeat later stations).

## W3 — "Dragon Longform" (45 × 40/20, full integration)

Warm-up (1–6): 1 march ramp · 2 ankle pumps + heel-toe rocks · 3 deepening
squats · 4 hinge + reach · 5 90/90 switches · 6 wall scapular reach.
Balance/skill (7–10): 7 single-leg balance + short-foot L · 8 same R ·
9 slow front kick L · 10 same R.
Strength (11–22): 11 split squat L · 12 R · 13 1.5-rep push-up (toe-safe) ·
14 single-leg RDL L · 15 R · 16 one-arm row L · 17 R · 18 half-kneeling
press L · 19 R · 20 single-leg glute bridge L · 21 R · 22 horse-stance
goblet hold.
Heavy tendon (23–26): 23 weighted straight-knee calf raise L · 24 R ·
25 seated soleus raise L · 26 R.
Cardio-chain (27–34): 27 knee-lift cardio · 28 DB swing (planted) ·
29 lateral step-and-reach L · 30 R · 31 knees-down inchworm walkout ·
32 foot-planted shadowboxing · 33 backward walking · 34 two-DB farmer carry.
Accessory physio / active ROM (35–42): 35 wall-press hip isometric L ·
36 R · 37 seated psoas raise slow-lower L · 38 R · 39 wall external-rotation
isometric L (5–8 s presses) · 40 R · 41 supported standing side-leg raise L
(3 s up, 2 s hold, 3 s down) · 42 R.

45 breathing + reach.

## W4 — "Crane Longform" (45 × 40/20, balance/active-flex emphasis)

Warm-up (1–7): 1 march + arm circles · 2 cat-cow · 3 controlled leg
pendulum L (small→bigger) · 4 same R · 5 lateral squat rock (controlled) ·
6 wall slides · 7 ankle pumps + heel-toe rocks.
Cardio-balance (8–13): 8 lateral step-to-balance L · 9 R (flat-foot, no
hop) · 10 crane-stance hold L (split into 2 holds if form fades) · 11 R ·
12 slow step-down L (3 s descent, pelvis level) · 13 R.
Kick skill (14–17): 14 slow side kick L · 15 R · 16 slow roundhouse L ·
17 R (wall ok, no forceful pivot).
Strength (18–29): 18 goblet squat 1.5-rep · 19 push-up plus (toe-safe) ·
20 lateral lunge L · 21 R · 22 one-arm snatch L (4–5 kg first, planted) ·
23 R · 24 two-arm bent-over row · 25 suitcase march (switch 20 s) ·
26 two-DB Romanian deadlift (3 s down, pause) · 27 single-leg glute
bridge L (long-lever option) · 28 R · 29 dead bug.
Physio (30–37): 30 weighted straight-knee calf L · 31 R · 32 seated soleus
raise L · 33 R · 34 hip hitch L · 35 R · 36 prone Y/W (unweighted, 2 s
holds) · 37 toe-spread + short-foot (~20 s/foot).
Cardio (38–39): 38 foot-planted shadowboxing · 39 brisk backward walking.
Active flexibility (40–41): 40 seated straddle active hinge (not passive) ·
41 supine active hamstring extension, alternating (3 s out / 3 s back).
Cooldown (42–45): 42 hip-flexor stretch L · 43 R · 44 side-bend holds
(20 s/side, symmetrical) · 45 breathing.

## Deliberately excluded (library, not default)

Copenhagen adductor (until right hip assessed), weighted Russian twist,
side plank pair, bear crawl/bear plank (toe+shoulder stacking), renegade
row, feet-elevated push-up, wall sit (redundant), side-lying ER with 4–5 kg
(too heavy — wall isometric instead), ballistic leg swings, standing
knee-raise finisher (psoas redundancy).

## Outside the app (reminder)

stations count toward them; 48–72 h between hard tendon days) — reference
card, not an app routine. These four routines are NOT the complete physio
system; the dedicated clinic routine remains a future option.
